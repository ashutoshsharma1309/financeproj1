# Reports

Generate profit & loss and category breakdowns from the Reports module
(`/reports`), then export them.

## Period

Choose **All time**, **This month**, **This quarter** or **This year**. The
report recomputes for the selected range.

## Profit & Loss

- Total income, total expenses, net profit and margin
- Income by category and expenses by category, each with a share of the total

Built by `buildProfitLoss` with an optional date range.

## Breakdown view

Toggle to **Breakdown** for revenue and expense donuts for the period.

## Export

- **CSV** — a flat table of income and expense categories with shares
- **PDF** — a print-ready profit & loss statement with KPIs and category tables

Every figure is derived from your entries; with no data the module shows an
empty state.
