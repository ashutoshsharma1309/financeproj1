# Expenses

Track spending by vendor and category from the Expenses module (`/expenses`).

## Adding an expense

Click **Add expense** and enter a vendor (e.g. "AWS"), amount and date.
Optionally assign an expense category and notes.

## Summary

- **Total expenses** across all entries
- **This month** — expenses dated in the current month
- **Average entry** — total divided by the number of records

## Categories

Only expense categories appear in the picker. Use categories to see your biggest
cost drivers in the dashboard donut and in reports.

## Export

Download all expenses as CSV (vendor, amount, date, category, notes).

## Where it flows

Expenses reduce net profit, add to cash-flow outflow, and appear in the expense
breakdown and profit & loss report.
