# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the finance terms used across AI CFO.

- **Revenue** — money your business earns from sales or services.
- **Expense** — money your business spends (vendors, payroll, tools, rent).
- **Net profit** — revenue minus expenses over a period.
- **Profit margin** — net profit as a percentage of revenue.
- **Cash position** — the net of your account balances right now.
- **Inflow / outflow** — cash coming in / going out over a period.
- **Burn** — the average net cash you consume per month when spending exceeds
  income.
- **Runway** — how many months your cash lasts at the current burn rate.
- **Invoice** — a bill sent to a client, with line items and tax.
- **Outstanding** — invoiced amounts not yet paid.
- **Overdue** — sent invoices past their due date.
- **Subtotal / tax / total** — line-item sum, tax applied, and the final amount.
- **Forecast** — a projection of future revenue, expenses and cash from
  assumptions you set.
- **P&L (profit & loss)** — a statement of income, expenses and net for a period.
- **Fiscal year** — your business's 12-month accounting period.
