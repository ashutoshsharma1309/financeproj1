# Revenue

Track every income source from the Revenue module (`/revenue`).

## Adding revenue

Click **Add revenue** and enter a source (e.g. "Product sales"), amount and
date. Optionally assign an income category and notes.

## Summary

- **Total revenue** across all entries
- **This month** — revenue dated in the current month
- **Entries** — the number of records

## Categories

Only income categories appear in the picker. Manage them under
[Company Settings](../src/app/(app)/company). Uncategorized revenue is still
counted in totals.

## Export

Download all revenue as CSV (source, amount, date, category, notes).

## Where it flows

Revenue contributes to `totalRevenue`, net profit, the dashboard trend chart,
cash-flow inflow and the revenue breakdown in reports.
